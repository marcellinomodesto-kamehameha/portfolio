About Me

My Story

My name is Marcellino Modesto, and I am currently 23 years old. I've lived in Sparks, Nevada my entire life. This is home and will always be home. I've grown up playing video games, and I still do to this day. It wasn't until I started playing web based games using Adobe Flash, when it was still alive, that I started questioning not only how video games are created, but how the internet and everything involving the web worked. I tried my hardest at a young age to understand.

Starting in elementary, I vividly remember using code.org. This was my first introduction to programming. Then, during middle school I took a robotics course. I remember we would have to build the robot and then program it to follow a line or move in a certain direction. I loved it and wanted more, I was getting closer to understanding. Then in highschool, my opportunity presented itself in the form of an electives class that was being offered. Web Design I.

My Journey

Starting web design was fascinating and was like nothing I've ever learned before. I was taught the basics using Adobe Dreamweaver. HTML and CSS was all we went over, with a very tiny amount of JavaScript. For the next three years, I took Web Design until I completed the final course. I was a junior when I finished, and it was also the last year the class was being offered at the school. I realized by that final year that this is what I wanted to pursue a career in.

By the time I graduated high school, I was offered basically a full-ride scholarship for whatever I wanted to do. Immediately after graduation, I enrolled at Truckee Meadows Community College (TMCC) and started pursuing my associate degree in Web Development. There I learned how to use the command-line interface (CLI), Linux, JavaScript, Project Management, Networking essentials, understanding the components of a computer, and much more. I graduated in 2025 with my associates in Web Development, and decided to go even further and work towards my bachelors. As of now, I am currently enrolled at Bellevue University, working towards getting my bachelor degree in May of 2027.

My Passion

I enjoy web development and learning how its different parts connect. From front-end development to back-end systems, I love seeing everything work together like pieces of a puzzle. Of course, development isn't as easy as putting together a puzzle, but the satisfaction of solving problems and building something functional is just as rewarding. My goal is to continue developing my skills, learning about current and emerging technologies, and eventually building a career as a full-stack web developer.
