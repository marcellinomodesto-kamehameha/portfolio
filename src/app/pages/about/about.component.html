About Me

My Story

My name is Marcellino Modesto, and I am currently 23 years old. I've lived in Sparks, Nevada my entire life, this is home and will always be home. I've grown up playing video games, and I still do till this day. It wasn't until I started playing web based games using Adobe Flash (When it was still alive) that I started questioning not only how video games are created, but how the internet and everything involving the web worked. I tried my hardest at a young age to understand.

Starting in elementary, I vividly remember using code.org. My first introduction. Then, during middle school I took a robotics course. I remember we would have to build the robot and then program it to follow a line or move in a certain direction. I loved it and wanted more, I was getting closer to understanding. Then in highschool, my opportunity presented itself in the form of an electives class that was being offered. Web Design I.

My Journey

Starting web design was fascinating and was like nothing I've ever learned before. I was taught the basics using Adobe Dreamweaver. Html and CSS was all we went over, with a very tiny amount of JavaScript. For the next three years, I took Web Design until I completed the final course. I was a junior when I finished, and it was also the last year the class was being offered at the school. I realized by that final year that this is what I wanted to pursue a career in.

By time I graduated high school, I was offered basically a full-ride scholarship for whatever I wanted to do. Immediately after graduation, I enrolled at Truckee Meadows Community College (TMCC) and started pursuing my associates degree in Web Development. There I learned how to use the CLI, Linux, JavaScript, Project Management, Networking essentials, understanding the components of a computer, and much more. I graduated in 2025 with my associates in Web Development, and decided to go even further and work towards my bachelors. As of now, I am currently enrolled at Bellevue University, working towards getting my bachelors degree in May of 2027.

My Passion

I enjoy web development and learning how everything connects. From the front-end of development to the back-end, I love watching it work together and see it connect like puzzle pieces. Granted, its not as easy as doing a puzzle, but the satisfaction is still there. My goal is to continue to hone my skills, learning current and future technology, and eventually build my career as a Full Stack Web Developer.
